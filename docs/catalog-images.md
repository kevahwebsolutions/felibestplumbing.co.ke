# Catalogue images

Source: 41 screen captures from WhatsApp Desktop (the app offers no download), 370 to 500 px wide. Every image was opened and inspected.
Processing (`npm run catalog:images`, `scripts/process-catalog-images.mts`): auto-orient, no upscaling at all (so the 1.5x limit is never reached), long edge capped at 1600 px, WebP quality 80. No border trim was applied: the captures are already cropped to the picture and have no uniform borders, so trimming could only cut into the product.
Kept 29 of 41 images. Excluded 12.

## Exceptions: kept although a price mark is visible

A rule of "no text that is not part of the product" would leave these products with no photo at all, and a product page without a photo is worse than one with a small price mark. They are flagged for the owner to replace first.

- front-load-washing-machine-cover-kes-800: the only photo shows an overlaid "@800" mark. It matches the current price. The other photo has handwriting.
- washing-machine-tap: single photo, small handwritten "650" on the tap.
- washing-machine-scent-beads (4 photos): "1000" handwritten on every bottle label.
- colour-and-dirt-catcher: front photo has a handwritten "1000" over the box; the back photo is clean but does not show the product front.
- washing-machine-inlet-pipe-3m: faint handwriting on the card label.
